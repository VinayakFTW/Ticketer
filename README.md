# About

Students and staff can raise various support, maintenance, and administrative tickets through a college helpdesk or grievance portal.

### IT and Technical Support Tickets

- Wi-Fi and Network: Issues connecting to the campus internet or campus VPN.
- Portal and LMS Access: Password resets or login errors for student portals, email, or online learning management systems.
- Lab Equipment: Broken computers, printer errors, or missing software in computer labs.

### Facilities and Maintenance Tickets

- Classroom Repairs: Broken desks, chairs, doors, or faulty projectors and microphones.
- Electrical and Plumbing: Leaking taps, broken lights, power failure, or non-working air conditioning in rooms and labs.
- Hostel Maintenance: Cleanliness issues, pest control, or room fixture repairs in student dorms.

### Academic and Administrative Tickets

- ID Cards: Requests for new, replacement, or updated student identity cards.
- Fee and Finance: Payment failures, receipt downloads, or scholarship status questions.
- Records and Certificates: Requests for transcripts, migration certificates, or bonafide letters.

### Student Services and Grievances

- Library Support: Book renewals, missing book reports, or fine disputes.
- Transport: Bus pass renewals or route timing complaints.
- General Complaints: Ragging reports, harassment issues, or formal grievance redresses directed to specific campus committees.

Ticketer works towards providing a faster resolution to these tickets/issues raised by users by automatically routing each ticket to concerned department and bumping them time-to-time for updates regarding the issue until the issue is marked as solved
